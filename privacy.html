Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Short version: we (the developer) don't collect anything about you: no accounts, no analytics of our own. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage information to show and measure ads. Kingfall Premium removes the ads. Online play is run by Apple's Game Center.

Information we collect

None. We don't run servers, accounts or analytics, and we never receive your name, contacts, location or anything else about you. The app plays offline, except for ads in the free version and online games.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your approximate location based on your IP address, the ads you see and interact with, and crash and performance information. This information goes to Google, not to us.

  • Your choice about tracking: the app asks whether it may track you, using Apple's standard prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but aren't personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EU, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, Google's consent form appears before any ads, and you can choose how your information is used.
  • No ads with Premium: buying Kingfall Premium turns off all ads and Google's ad software in the app stops loading ads.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (such as the background, board view, difficulty and monster team) and your game statistics (wins, losses and draws) locally on your device so they're there the next time you play. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Online play (Game Center)

Online games are run entirely by Apple's Game Center. If you choose to play online, Game Center uses your Game Center account and nickname to match you with other players, and stores each game's moves on Apple's servers so both players can take their turns. We never see or store your Apple account, contacts or any other personal information, and we receive nothing from these games. You can play without Game Center, and you can turn Game Center off in your device's Settings at any time. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Kingfall Premium) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you.

Children

Kingfall is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are provided by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
